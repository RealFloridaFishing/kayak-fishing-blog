## Mosquito Lagoon: A Strong Morning Bite

I launched at Beacon 42 at 7:00 a.m. on November 12 and headed out into Mosquito Lagoon. The bite was hot through the morning, with fish holding tight along the shorelines. A dark 3.5" paddle tail was the most productive lure of the day.

By the time the action slowed around 11:30 a.m., I had caught five fish: four redfish—including one slot fish and one overslot fish—and a snook. It was a rewarding morning on the water, with the Lagoon fish noticeably more active than the fish I had encountered the week before.

The air temperature was in the mid-60s, and the barometer read 29.88 inHg. The wind was around 8 mph from the west, shifting to the northwest around noon. A cold front moved through that evening, making the morning's productive pre-frontal window a welcome one.

## The Previous Week on Sykes Creek

The previous week, I fished Sykes Creek in the Banana River. That outing was more of a grind, with fish holding tight to shoreline cover as well. I caught four trout and got broken off by an overslot redfish that I sight casted with a jerk shad.  I believe my line must have been frayed - the fish ran into open water and I was using a heavier than usual 30lb leader since I knew i'd be fishing mangrove lines.  At least I got a nice drag pulling run out of it before it snapped off.  

The bite has most likely cooled off due to the cold front, but it's worth a shot getting out there if you get a chance.  I would start on the shorelines near open water and if they aren't still there, try the nearest drop-off or backwaters with dark/muddy bottom - they probably didn't move too far.
